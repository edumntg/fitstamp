FitStamp

Stamps every LinkedIn job with how well it matches you.

1 API key

Scoring runs on Jev 1.13, a model by TypeSafe. Use a key from either place; a job costs about $0.0001 on both.

Where your key is from

Create a key at openrouter.ai/settings/keys. Requests go through OpenRouter to TypeSafe and are billed to your OpenRouter credits.

Create a key at console.typesafe.ai/keys. Requests go straight to TypeSafe's API and are billed to your TypeSafe account.

2 Your resume

PDF, TXT or Markdown. The text is extracted here in your browser; check it below and fix anything that came out garbled.

3 LinkedIn profile optional, improves accuracy

FitStamp opens your profile in a tab, reads it, stores the text on this computer and closes the tab. Leave the field empty to use the profile you're logged in with.
